# Gazebo survey patrol, 6 drones with cameras (run with --gazebo --time-scale 0.3 and the layout from
# `python3 -m sitl.patrol_layout console-args`; world from sitl/launch_gazebo_patrol.sh).
#   1. centralized: the V flies a square patrol; the leader meets a pole on the first edge
#   2. decentralized: three pairs spread over the survey points, then move to the other three (two survey phases)
#   3. hybrid: pairs again, the coordinator is cut, the swarm carries on
#   4. regroup: back to the origin in the original V, land
# Waits are wall-clock seconds at ~0.3x real time. `capture` saves a frame from every drone's camera.
wait swarm ready 1800
send launch
poll 6 1500 airborne armed=yes
sleep 20
send capture start_V
send mode centralized
expect Control mode is now CENTRALIZED 300
send patrol square 10 0.4
sleep 70
send capture corner_NE
sleep 40
send capture pole_pass
sleep 40
send capture corner_NW
send route stop
sleep 15
status
send mode decentralized
expect Control mode is now DECENTRALIZED 300
send formation pairs
expect Formation transition to PAIRS complete 600
sleep 10
send capture pairs_A
send formation pairs_b
expect Formation transition to PAIRS_B complete 600
sleep 10
send capture pairs_B
send mode hybrid
expect Control mode is now HYBRID 300
send formation pairs
expect Formation transition to PAIRS complete 600
send capture hybrid_pairs_A
send coord off
sleep 25
send capture hybrid_coordinator_lost
send coord on
sleep 10
status
send goto 0 0
sleep 70
send formation v
expect Formation transition to V complete 600
sleep 15
send capture regrouped_V
status
health
send land
sleep 120
status
