# 6 drones in Gazebo (run with --gazebo --time-scale 0.16 --control-mode hybrid): takeoff, a synchronized formation
# change, in-flight mode switches through all three modes, landing. Wall-clock waits are long because the
# simulator runs at ~0.16x real time.
wait swarm ready 1800
send launch
poll 6 1500 airborne armed=yes
sleep 40
status
send formation line
expect Formation transition to LINE complete 600
sleep 30
status
send mode decentralized
expect Control mode is now DECENTRALIZED 300
sleep 60
status
send mode centralized
expect Control mode is now CENTRALIZED 300
sleep 60
send formation v
expect Formation transition to V complete 600
sleep 30
status
health
send land
sleep 240
status
